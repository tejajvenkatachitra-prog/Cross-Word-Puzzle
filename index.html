Algorithm notes

MRV heuristic + forward checking over Trie-backed candidates

Each step picks the unfilled slot with the fewest legal words left (Minimum Remaining Values), looks up candidates in a Trie by pattern (fixed letters + wildcards), places one, and immediately checks every crossing slot still has options left — backtracking the instant one doesn't.
